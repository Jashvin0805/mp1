Fun Facts

A lion's roar is so lound that it can be heard up to 5 miles away. Lions always love to sleep - they sleep about 20 hours a day, which helps conserve their energy for hunting.

Zebras have a unique black-and-white stripe patterns that act just like human fingerprints. Zebras have the ability to sleep while standing up due to a locking mechanism in their leg joints.

Elephants are the largest living land mammals on Earth. Elephants can hear thorugh their feet by picking up low-frequency vibrations traveling through the ground.
